# Student Diagnostic Checklist
**Workshop 1: IT Troubleshooting Challenge**

Use this checklist during every incident to ensure you are following the correct troubleshooting methodology. Do not skip steps.

- [ ] I can reproduce the reported problem.
- [ ] I have recorded the exact error message or symptom.
- [ ] I have checked the relevant system or network status.
- [ ] I have collected evidence before changing anything.
- [ ] I have written down a testable hypothesis.
- [ ] I have changed only what was necessary and had approval where required.
- [ ] I have repeated the original test after the fix.
- [ ] I have checked that the fix has not caused a new problem.
- [ ] I have recorded commands, outputs and the final result.

# Assessment Rubric — Workshop 1: IT Troubleshooting Challenge
**Practical Approaches to IT Projects and Problem-Solving | Alijó IT Workshops**

Use this rubric to evaluate teams based on their methodology, technical execution, documentation, and teamwork during the lab.

## Grading Scale

* **4 - Excellent:** Exceeds expectations; independent, structured, and highly accurate.
* **3 - Good:** Meets expectations; follows the process with minor guidance.
* **2 - Needs Improvement:** Struggles with the process; relies heavily on guessing or teacher intervention.
* **1 - Unacceptable:** Fails to follow instructions; violates safety rules or causes further system damage.

---

## Evaluation Criteria

| Category | Excellent (4) | Good (3) | Needs Improvement (2) | Unacceptable (1) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Methodology & Process** | Strictly follows the 7-step troubleshooting process. Never guesses. Always gathers evidence before acting. | Follows the process most of the time. Occasionally jumps to conclusions but self-corrects. | Frequently skips steps (e.g., tries to fix before diagnosing). Relies on trial and error. | Completely ignores the methodology. Changes random settings hoping for a fix. |
| **Diagnostic Skills** | Uses correct CLI commands efficiently. Accurately interprets terminal outputs and logs to find the root cause. | Uses mostly correct commands. May need minor hints to interpret complex logs or outputs. | Struggles to remember or find basic diagnostic commands. Misinterprets clear error messages. | Unable to perform basic diagnostics (e.g., cannot check IP or ping). Refuses to use the terminal. |
| **Resolution & Verification** | Implements the correct fix safely (one change at a time). Defines and executes precise verification tests. | Implements a working fix but might make multiple changes at once. Verification is basic but functional. | Fixes the issue only after teacher intervention or breaks something else in the process. Verification is forgotten. | Fails to resolve the incidents. Implements reckless changes using `sudo` without understanding them. |
| **Documentation (Reporting)** | Incident logs are highly detailed, including exact timestamps, commands used, raw terminal outputs, and clear explanations. | Logs are complete and readable but may lack some specific terminal outputs or minor details. | Logs are incomplete, vague, or written after the fact rather than during the incident. | Documentation is missing, entirely illegible, or contains falsified information. |
| **Teamwork & Roles** | Rotates roles flawlessly. Communicates technical findings clearly. Every member participates equally. | Good collaboration. Roles are mostly respected, though one or two members might dominate slightly. | Poor communication. Roles are ignored. Some team members are completely disengaged. | Dysfunctional team dynamics. Arguing, working individually instead of as a team, or ignoring safety rules. |
